FOOD ALLERGY NOTICE

Our baskets may include foods that contain, or were made in facilities that handle, common allergens such as milk, eggs, wheat (gluten), soy, peanuts, tree nuts, sesame, fish and shellfish. Packaged items arrive sealed with their original ingredient labels. Please tell us about any allergies when you order, and we will gladly adjust your basket, but we cannot guarantee that any basket is completely allergen-free.

How to order

  1. Add baskets to your cart from the menu, or describe a custom one in the form.
  2. Send the form with your date and card message. We'll confirm within a day.
  3. Pay by Cash App, Venmo or Square, then pick up in San Antonio at no charge, or choose local delivery. Delivery rates vary by distance.

Please order at least 3 days ahead. Holiday orders close December 15.

OUR STORY

From Mexico to San Antonio, con cariño

Casa Eme is a Latina-owned business born from a simple passion: bringing happiness and joy to the people around us.

Our roots stretch from Mexico all the way to San Antonio, and every basket carries that warmth. Each one is thoughtfully chosen, wrapped by hand and made to celebrate the people and moments that matter most.
