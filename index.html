How to order

  1. Pick a basket from the menu, or ask for a custom one.
  2. Send the form with your date and card message. We'll confirm within a day.
  3. Pay by Cash App, Venmo or Square, then pick up in San Antonio or get local delivery for a small fee.

Please order at least 3 days ahead. Holiday orders close December 15.
